About Me

I graduated from Glasgow Caledonian University with a BSc (Hons) in Games Development (2:1), and I’m currently undertaking an MSc in User Experience and Interaction Design. My interests sit across game development, interface design and the way people interact with digital products.

My work includes collaborative gameplay development on Ascension, the solo-developed platformer TimeHopper, and ongoing Unity projects including a football management simulation and Dust Haven, a mobile survival game. I’ve also developed a management app for a youth football club, shaped by the practical needs of coaches and parents.

These projects have given me experience taking ideas through design, implementation, testing and iteration. I’m looking for opportunities in games development, UX and interactive digital products where I can keep building on both my technical and user-centred design skills.
